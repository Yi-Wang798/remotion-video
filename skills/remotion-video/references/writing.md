# Natural video writing

Adapted from the local Humanizer and Humanizer ZH skills; see `NOTICE.md` for sources and licenses. Apply to narration, captions, labels and descriptions. Keep editorial notes outside the film.

Use direct verbs and concrete objects. Explain what happens in this scene. Preserve facts, names and meaningful qualifications. Do not invent evidence, measurements, authority or personal experience to sound natural.

Read the script as speech for its intended audience. Vary sentence length with the thought. Keep humor and personality where they fit. Avoid forced excitement, formulaic introductions, repeated three-item lists, decorative English subtitles and rhetorical “not X, but Y” constructions.

Every sentence should explain, identify or advance the intended experience. Match the opening to the purpose: a direct result can suit a briefing; a developing event can suit a story. Atmosphere may use silence and sparse text. Keep humor, suspense and questions where they belong rather than applying them to every format. Delete production chatter, self-praise and obvious descriptions of the medium. Do not automatically add:

- 拟人化科普动画 / 非真实显微影像 / AI-generated illustration.
- 可爱外表 × 真实原理 or a generic “让科学原理动起来” slogan.
- Repeated notes about every cartoon's quantities, scale or shape when the viewer has no reason to interpret them as measurements.
- “接下来我们将深入探讨”, “值得注意的是”, or “In conclusion” when the next sentence already conveys the point.

Keep scientific statements that change understanding. For example, “有些噬菌体可以先进入溶原状态” explains an alternative and belongs in the story. Source citations, authoring notes and QA records normally go in accompanying documentation. Honor a requested credit sequence or a specific necessary disclosure.

Edit the script before recording, then once more against the actual visuals. Do not read every label aloud or add a paragraph explaining an already clear metaphor. After placing captions, check duplication and reading time. Keep names for recurring objects stable.

Inspect the actual overlays as well as `screenText` in the plan to catch leftover template footers. Do not preserve paragraph counts or draft length. Delete an empty sentence instead of expanding it into a different empty sentence. Deliver finished copy; keep draft/audit chatter in the review file.

Use version 4 `overlays` as the complete non-caption text inventory. Page counters, invented series headers, repeated topic footers and decorative microcopy are off by default. Label the object or relationship viewers need to understand; remove the extra sentence that simply repeats the narration. A requested brand or chapter treatment is a deliberate exception, recorded in the plan.
